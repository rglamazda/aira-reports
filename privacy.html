Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

1. Who we are

AIra Reports is a YouTube channel about artificial intelligence news, presented by AIra, an AI presenter, and operated by Acceptserve Inc. (“we”). The AIra Reports publisher (“the app”) is the channel's private production tool: it prepares the channel's videos and uploads them to the channel's own YouTube account. The app is not offered to the public. The only person who signs in to it with Google is the owner of the AIra Reports channel.

2. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and thumbnails to the AIra Reports channel. Data sent to and received from YouTube is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy (www.google.com/policies/privacy). Use of the app is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service; see our Terms of Service.

3. Information the app accesses and how it is used

When the channel owner connects the channel, Google asks them to allow the app to manage their YouTube account (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube). With that permission the app only:

  • reads the connected channel's ID, title, address and picture, to show which channel is connected;
  • uploads the channel's own videos with their title, description, tags, category, language, privacy setting, scheduled time and AI-content (“altered or synthetic content”) label;
  • sets the custom thumbnail of a video it uploaded.

The app does not read or collect viewers' data, comments, subscribers, analytics, watch history, messages, email addresses or contacts, and it does not access any other YouTube user's data.

For each upload the app keeps a production record on the channel's private server: the YouTube video ID, the title, description and tags it sent, the privacy setting YouTube applied and the upload time. It is used only to show the editor what was published.

4. How the information is stored and protected

  • The Google sign-in (OAuth refresh token) is stored on the channel's private server in a file readable only by the app's own system account. It is not stored in a database, not written to logs, never sent to a web browser and never shared.
  • Short-lived access tokens are kept only in memory, for at most one hour.
  • The server is on a private network and is not reachable from the internet.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any information with third parties. Information leaves the server only when the app sends a video and its details to YouTube, at the channel owner's request. The app has no advertising and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

6. Cookies and information on your device

The app does not place cookies on, or store or read information from, the devices of viewers or of any YouTube user. People who watch AIra Reports videos interact only with YouTube, whose handling of their data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

7. Keeping and deleting information

  • Disconnecting the channel in the app (Settings → YouTube → Disconnect) revokes the app's access at Google and deletes the stored sign-in and channel details immediately.
  • If access is revoked through Google instead, the stored sign-in stops working at once and is deleted, together with any other data obtained from YouTube API Services, within 7 days.
  • Upload records are kept as the channel's production log for as long as the channel uses the app, and are deleted on request within 7 days.

8. Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the app's access to their Google account at any time on Google's security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

9. Children

The app is a production tool for the channel's owner and is not directed at children. AIra Reports videos are uploaded as “not made for kids”.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date. The full history of changes is public in this site's repository.

11. Contact

Questions or requests about this policy or your data: open an issue at github.com/rglamazda/aira-reports/issues.
