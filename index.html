AIra Reports

AIra Reports is a YouTube channel about artificial intelligence news, presented by AIra, an AI presenter, and operated by Acceptserve Inc.

The AIra Reports publisher

The AIra Reports publisher is the channel's private production tool. It turns approved news stories into short videos and uploads them to the AIra Reports channel through YouTube API Services:

  • the channel owner connects the channel once with Google sign-in;
  • for each finished video, the app drafts a title, description, tags and thumbnail; a human editor reviews and edits them;
  • the editor chooses the privacy (private, unlisted, public or scheduled) and uploads the video, which is labelled as altered or synthetic (AI-made) content;
  • each description credits the news sources, the music and the tools used.

The app uploads only to the channel it is connected to. It does not read viewers' data, comments or analytics, and it shares nothing with third parties. See the Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
